Ningshing Che Organization respects your privacy and gives users control over their personal information.

If you have created an account in the Ningshing Che Android application and wish to stop using our services, you can request the permanent deletion of your account and associated personal data.

## How to Request Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your Ningshing Che account by following these steps:

1. Visit our official website: [https://ningshingche.com/](https://ningshingche.com/)
2. Navigate to the [Contact Us](/about-us) section, or write directly to [ningshingche@gmail.com](mailto:ningshingche@gmail.com?subject=Ningshing%20Che%20Account%20Deletion%20Request).
3. Send us an account deletion request using your registered email address.
4. Include "Ningshing Che Account Deletion Request" in the subject line.
5. Provide the email address associated with your Ningshing Che account.

Our team may contact you to verify your account ownership before processing your request.

## What Information Will Be Deleted?

After your account deletion request is verified and processed, we will delete or arrange the deletion of personal information associated with your account, where applicable, including:

- Account profile information, such as your name and email address.
- Account authentication identifiers.
- Personal profile information.
- User-submitted content associated with your account, where applicable.
- Comments, messages, and other account-associated contributions, where applicable.
- Other personal information that is no longer required for legitimate purposes.

## What Information May Be Retained?

Certain information may need to be retained for a limited period when required for:

- Legal and regulatory compliance.
- Security and fraud prevention.
- Resolving disputes.
- Enforcing applicable terms and policies.
- Maintaining legitimate business records.

Any retained information will be restricted to the relevant purpose and deleted when it is no longer necessary.

**Retention period:** up to 90 days after your request has been processed, unless a longer period is required by law.

## Account Deletion Processing Time

We will review your request, verify account ownership where necessary, and process eligible deletion requests within 30 days of receiving them.

We will notify you when your request has been processed or if additional information is required.

Uninstalling the Ningshing Che application does not automatically delete your account or associated personal information.

You must submit an account deletion request using the process described above.
